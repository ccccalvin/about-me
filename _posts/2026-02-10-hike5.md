# thoughts

day 1:
- Woke up at Josiah’s place and left early to grab a PLB, but misjudged the distance to Clarke Gorge so we went straight to Charlotte’s Pass instead
- Parked near Main Ridge Walk, last toilet stop before hiking, and somehow I already lost one of my Nike slides
- Planned to stay at Mt Twynam but camped along the ridge instead; felt surprisingly strong by the end of the day
- Wind smashing the tent all night sounded like someone walking around outside. Went out at 2:30am to pee and the sky was insanely clear — beautiful but freezing

day 2:
- Missed sunrise; Josiah was already awake eating Indo mie
- Cooked inside the tent because the wind was too strong, then packed up slowly
- Forecast said thunderstorm so we skipped peaks and set up early at Wilkinson’s Creek
- Climbed some boulders while Josiah renamed every climbing term into something completely wrong
- Walked back alone to the ridge for reception to message family; kept thinking someone was behind me but it was just my hair brushing my jacket
- Found a camp spot, collected creek water, and had one of the worst outdoor poops of my life because the ground was too hard to dig
- Spent the afternoon waiting for a storm that never really came, just thinking a lot about home and realising I’m not as fearless about solitude as I thought
- Heavy rain hit at night around 2:30am — loud but peaceful in a strange way

day 3:
- Woke up to thick fog, made mac and cheese, packed slowly while tents dried
- Summitted Mt Kosciuszko with absolutely no view thanks to the fog
- Had lunch at a really nice spot someone walked past and complimented
- Saw the forecast dropping to 5°C and decided we were not built for that, so we bailed back to the car to buy more layers and move camp
- Drove at night down a “4WD Required” road in a 20-year-old Mitsubishi Lancer, nearly hit rabbits and a horse, but somehow made it to Magpie Flat

day 4:
- Woke freezing early while Josiah slept peacefully, snoring in his new merino layers
- Tried fishing at Clarke Gorge but gave up after hearing we’d need to cross eight rivers; zero fish anyway
- Returned to the car with soaked boots and changed on the spot
- Stopped at Canberra for Subway — genuinely elite
- Got home around 8pm feeling tired, thankful, and slightly more respectful of mountains

peak moments:

- "The girly touch"
